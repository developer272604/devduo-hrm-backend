# HRM MASTER SPECIFICATION

## PHASE 3 — ATTENDANCE, SHIFTS & TIME OFFICE

Continue from completed Phase 1 and Phase 2.

The product remains:

- Multi-country from Day 1
- Multi-tenant SaaS
- Enterprise-capable
- Designed to compete with leading HRMS platforms such as Keka
- API-first
- Permission-aware
- Workflow-ready
- Audit-first
- Mobile-responsive
- Automation-ready

Do NOT rebuild Phase 1 or Phase 2 foundations.

Reuse:

- Authentication
- Tenant isolation
- RBAC
- Data scopes
- Field-level permissions
- Organization structures
- Employee master
- Effective-dated employee assignments
- Design system
- Application shell
- My Work
- Notifications
- Audit framework
- Employee 360
- People Action Center
- Organization Memory
- Domain events
- Settings framework
- Country configuration

Do NOT start:

- Payroll processing
- Full Leave Management
- Recruitment
- Performance
- Learning
- Expenses

However, attendance must be designed so Payroll and Leave can consume finalized attendance data later.
